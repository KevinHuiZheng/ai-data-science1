Global warming is causing Earth’s average temperature to rise, leading to changes in weather, ecosystems, and sea levels. My project will investigate how global temperatures have changed over time and what factors, such as greenhouse gas emissions, contribute to these changes. This topic is important because understanding global warming can help people make informed decisions about reducing its effects and protecting the environment. I expect to use data such as historical temperature records, carbon dioxide levels, and greenhouse gas emissions to identify trends and relationships.
